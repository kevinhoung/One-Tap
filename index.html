Demo assumptions
  • Recommended panel count ≈ 35% of Google’s max packing (capped below the max), to mirror typical contractor proposals.
  • Yearly production scales from Google’s max-array yearly energy by panel count × panel wattage vs Google’s model panel.
  • Installed price uses flat $/W demo rates (Basic $2.40/W, Premium $3.15/W, Luxe $3.90/W) — not market quotes.
  • Savings assume $0.16 / kWh retail electricity and a simple payback (price ÷ annual bill savings). No incentives, financing, or degradation modeled.
  • This is a product demo only — not engineering, design, or pricing advice.
